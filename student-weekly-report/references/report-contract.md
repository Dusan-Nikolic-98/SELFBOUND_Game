# Report contract and default evaluation

Use this contract for every week. Weekly material changes the content, never the structure.

## Required report structure

1. **Osnovne informacije:** full name, email, Discord handle, week, pair/team or `nije primenljivo`, learner's specific contribution/role, submission date, and approved evidence references or an honest absence statement.
2. **Moj status:** one plain-text line in the exact form `**Status:** <vrednost>`, where `<vrednost>` is exactly one of `Završeno`, `Delimično završeno`, `Blokirano`, or `Nije započeto`, followed by a factual explanation.
3. **Rad ove nedelje:** one coherent first-person account covering applicable task/criteria, work attempted, individual contribution, verification and result, failure/blocker/limitation/learning, AI use plus human verification or explicit AI non-use, and tutor help needed or explicit non-need.
4. **Sledeći korak:** one observable, bounded action with a clear completion point. If none is planned before Session 2, say so and identify the next expected action point.
5. **Poverljiva napomena za tutora:** optional; use `Nema dodatne napomene` when empty.

Do not add learner self-assessment, week-specific fields, private links, or a scoring rubric.

## Minimum fallback

Keep identity and week, then include:

- status;
- primary evidence reference/description or explicit absence;
- three brief facts about work or attempts;
- verification/result or explicit `provera nije završena`;
- help needed or `pomoć trenutno nije potrebna`;
- one bounded next action.

Mark absent required facts `(**NEDOSTAJE:** kratak opis)`; never fill them plausibly. Keep a minimum draft readable: group related omissions in one marker under the affected section instead of repeating a marker in every empty header cell. Do not place questions, safety instructions, inspection notes, or other skill-process commentary in the report.

The confidential tutor note contains only a learner-supplied confidential note or `Nema dodatne napomene`. A request for support belongs in the weekly narrative.

## Default private evaluation

Evaluate every draft outside the report. Use `Spremno za potvrdu`, `Potrebna dopuna`, or `Minimalni nacrt sa vidljivim prazninama`, then list only actionable findings.

Check:

| Check | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Grounding | Personal claims come from the learner; observations are not upgraded into authorship, understanding, intent, or historical execution. |
| Required data | Identity, week, team context, date, status, contribution, references, next action, and tutor note state are present. |
| Consistency | Status, narrative, evidence, limitations, and next action do not contradict one another. |
| Evidence | Claimed completion/verification names a result or honestly states what is not evidenced. |
| Personal contribution | Pair/team work distinguishes the learner's action from shared output. |
| AI accountability | Meaningful use and human checking are stated, or the learner explicitly says AI was not used. |
| Support and next action | Help need is explicit; the next action is observable and bounded. |
| Adequacy | Content is specific and complete enough; length is assessed only as context for relevance, repetition, and missing substance. |
| Safety/privacy | No private links, obvious secrets, unnecessary sensitive data, or confidential-note leakage. State that visual review is not a guaranteed secret scan. |
| Standalone output | Serbian Markdown is readable in email without repository context or authoring instructions. |

Do not score, rank, predict a grade, or infer capability. Use `nije dokazano u dostavljenom izveštaju` for absent evidence.

## Compact Serbian interaction language

- Context: `Ako imaš materijal sesije ili nedeljni zadatak, priloži ga ili navedi dostupnu putanju. Mogu nastaviti i bez toga, ali neću moći da proverim usklađenost sa konkretnim zadatkom.`
- Inspection offer: `Ako želiš, mogu samo za potrebe dokaza da pregledam navedeni lokalni repozitorijum bez izmena. Pregled nije dokaz autorstva ili razumevanja. Da li da to uradim?`
- Pushback: `Mogu da uredim i proverim tvoj stvarni opis, ali ne mogu da izmislim šta si uradio/la, proverio/la, naučio/la ili kako si koristio/la AI.`
- Confirmation: `Pročitaj ceo nacrt i ispravi netačnosti. Ako je ovo tvoj potpun i tačan prikaz, potvrdi da mogu da ga označim kao finalan.`
- Safety: `Uklonio/la sam vrednost koja može biti tajna. Nemoj je ponovo slati. Ako je bila stvarna, odmah obavesti tutora i rotiraj ili opozovi kredencijal.`
